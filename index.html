Privacy Policy
Effective: April 6, 2026 · COPPA Compliant

1. Who We Are

MathCrown operates the platform at mymathcrown.com — a K-12 competitive math learning platform. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, especially children under 13.

2. Information We Collect

When you register we collect: first name, last name, grade level, date of birth, email address, and password. We also collect gameplay data (scores, battles, time spent) used solely to improve your learning experience.

Parents/Guardians: email address, password, and child account link code.

3. COPPA — Children Under 13

MathCrown complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). For children under 13:

  • We require verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information.
  • Parents may review, edit, or delete their child's data at any time by emailing [email protected].
  • We do not serve behavioral advertising to children under 13.
  • We never sell, rent, or share children's personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.
  • Children's accounts are linked to a parent account for oversight and prize redemption.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide personalized math practice and AI tutoring via Axiom
  • To display leaderboards and track progress
  • To send account-related emails (password reset, weekly reports)
  • To process prize redemptions through verified parent accounts

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore) with enterprise-grade encryption in transit and at rest. We use Firebase Authentication for secure login and never store plain-text passwords.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase — Authentication and database storage
  • Anthropic Claude API — Powers the Axiom AI math tutor
  • Web3Forms — Email capture for the founding family waitlist

7. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request account and data deletion within 30 days
  • Opt-out: Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any right, email [email protected]. We respond within 5 business days.

8. Contact Us

MathCrown Privacy Team — [email protected]
